# Chaitanya Portfolio | Design Brief

- **Design read:** A computer-science student and aspiring software engineer presents practical AI, ML, and full-stack work to recruiters who value clarity, craft, and evidence.
- **Concept spine:** Orbital systems lab. Each project is a system in motion, connected by thin trajectories, measured grids, and a central engineered object.
- **Delivery tier:** spectacle-lite: cinematic scroll-scrub film, layered CSS depth, responsive interactions, and restrained motion. No dependency on runtime AI services.
- **Locked palette:** Midnight ink `#11151A`, cloud white `#F3F4F2`, cool slate `#9AA4AF`, muted rose `#C77D8B` (single accent). The low-saturation rose distinguishes the work without the overused neon-blue / purple glow.
- **Locked type:** Outfit display + IBM Plex Mono technical labels, system sans fallback for body.
Animation mode: animated-website
- **Animation mode:** animated-website.
Journey shape: single-shot
- **Journey shape:** single-shot. One uninterrupted orbital-core animation gives the scroll a continuous visual thread and avoids distracting scene cuts.
Journey: (1) Establishing orbit: “Build with intent.” (2) Camera closes in on a precision wireframe core: the work is grounded in real systems. (3) Orbit lines converge and reveal depth: connect software, data, and product thinking. (4) The core opens into a clean final beauty frame: invite recruiters to explore projects and contact Chaitanya.
- **World grammar:** deep midnight studio, fine drafting grid, graphite surfaces, matte white and muted rose highlights, slow orbital movement, stable exposure, no rendered text, no logos, no watermarks.
- **Mobile framing:** keep the central orbital form within the middle 60% of the frame; use a centered cover crop and reduce motion when requested.
- **Delivery budget:** one compact H.264 MP4 and exact first-frame poster; target under 8 MiB desktop and mobile.
- **Section plan:** cinematic hero; asymmetric project index; editorial about split; compact skill matrix; milestone timeline; certification list; contact form/footer.
- **Asset plan:** bespoke procedural orbital film/poster, CSS depth scene, monogram mark, OG image, SVG favicon. External project screenshots and official certification logos are not supplied, so project visuals remain authored diagrams and credentials are explicit placeholders.
- **CTA inventory:** “Explore projects” (primary), “Resume PDF” (secondary, placeholder until uploaded), “Contact Chaitanya” (contact jump), “View repository” (only when a real URL is available).
- **Honesty constraints:** no invented metrics, employers, certifications, awards, GPA, or social profiles. Mark missing links and dates as TODOs.
